# Intro 

Hello! My name is Jacob, and I am currently a Senior Security Engineer at Cisco. My main focus is securing our cloud environments through and aligning them towards FedRamp/DoD standards. My passion comes from automating and building out a secure cloud to ensure the compliance and integrity of our cloud services!

# My experience. 
I first started off in High School, at first I wanted to be a muscician (I played Violin, Trumpet, and French Horn) but I wanted money and to be loved so I chose to go the Engineering route. 

I tolerated Cyberpatriot and I was kind of decent at it, but I really liked Networking so I pursued that and wanted to become a network engineer. I got my CCNA in high school and some random certs while working internships at Tepia Co where I did some Unity programming and software engineering, then at ViewSonic where i focused more on IT support. 

In College I realized certs don't really matter and focused more on projects, competitions. Freshman year I joined CCDC as the Networking lead, and CPTC as an Alternate. It was just after COVID so I met a lot of cool people, I would call my lifelong friends. 

CCDC we barely got to regionals (the team didn't the year before) but we got last place. A very humbling experience, but I got my first real cybersecurity internship at SoCal Edison which helped me land my internship in San Francisco at Cisco Meraki. Then at CIsco :D. 

The last years of my college years were dedicated to giving back to the SWIFT community as President, and MISSA as VP, helping facilitate hands on workshops for students.
